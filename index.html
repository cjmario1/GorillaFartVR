💨 GORILLA FART

Swing your arms and push off the ground, walls and trees to move. Press A / B / X / Y to rip a fart boost in the direction you're looking.

On a computer: WASD to move, Space to jump (hold it against walls and trees to climb), and F, Shift or click to fart.

Hit Play to drop into a random room. Use the computer up in the big tree to change your name, join a friend's room code, and pick Casual or Infection.

Climb the staircase up the giant tree, check out the computer, then drop into the hole to explore the caves. Walk into the glowing portal to climb back out. The caves have gorillas too, so watch out! Glowing portals out in the forest lead to the city, beach, heaven and hell, and in Infection there's a cure lab too.

Tap Enable mic first if you want voice chat (you can still listen without it).
