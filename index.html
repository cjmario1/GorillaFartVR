💨 GORILLA FART

Swing your arms and push off the ground, walls and trees to move. Press A / B / X / Y to rip a fart boost in the direction you're looking.

On a computer: WASD to move, Space to jump (hold it against walls and trees to climb), and F, Shift or click to fart.

Casual: chill out with colorful gorillas that just wander around.
Infection: red gorillas hunt you down. Survive as long as you can!

Climb the staircase up the giant tree, check out the computer, then drop into the hole to explore the caves. Walk into the glowing portal to climb back out. The caves have gorillas too, so watch out! A glowing orange portal out in the forest leads to the city.

Friends: type the same room code to play together. Leave it blank to play solo. Tap Enable mic first if you want voice chat (you can still listen without it).
